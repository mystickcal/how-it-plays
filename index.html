Techniques

10 techniques

  1. Coyote timeFor a short moment after the player runs off a ledge, jump still works as if they were on the ground.
  2. Jump bufferingIf the player presses jump just before landing, remember the press for a short time and jump the moment they touch the ground.
  3. Variable jump heightTap jump for a short hop, hold it for a full jump. When the player lets go while still rising, cut the upward speed.
  4. Camera lookahead and smoothingThe camera aims a bit ahead of where the player is facing and eases toward that point instead of being glued to the player.
  5. Input buffering for attacksA press made during an attack's recovery is saved and fires the moment the attack ends, instead of being thrown away.
  6. Invincibility frames with flickerAfter taking a hit, the player can't be hurt again for a short time, and the sprite flickers so they know.
  7. HitstopFreeze the action for a few frames when a hit lands so the impact registers.
  8. Screen shake with falloffShake the camera on impact, with the strength fading over time instead of a constant jitter.
  9. Acceleration and decelerationSpeed ramps up to max and back down to zero over a short time instead of snapping. The balance between the two decides whether movement feels tight or slippery.
  10. KnockbackA hit pushes the target away from the attacker with a small upward pop, then friction slows it down.
