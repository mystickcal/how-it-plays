Dash with cooldown

Also called: dash, air dash, dash timer, burst move

A short burst of fixed speed in the facing direction, with a cooldown so it can't be chained into flying.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · C K or Shift dash

Try this: Press C (or K, or Shift) to dash. Mash it with the cooldown on and off and compare dashes to presses.

What it does

A dash sets speed to a fixed value for a short time and ignores gravity. Without a limit, players chain dashes and skip the level. A cooldown blocks the next dash for a moment. Celeste also allows only one air dash until you land, which is a separate rule you can add on top.

Use it when

Action games that want a burst of speed or an escape move.

Watch out

Show the cooldown with a meter or a color change, or players will think the game dropped their input.

Starting values

Celeste's public player code dashes at 240 (480 px/s at this demo's size) for 0.15 seconds, with a 0.2 second cooldown that starts when the dash begins. It also freezes the game for 0.05 seconds as a dash starts. This demo starts its cooldown when the dash ends.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add a dash with a cooldown.
- On the dash button, if the dash is ready: set horizontal speed to facing * 480 px/s and vertical speed to 0 for 150 ms, with no gravity.
- When the dash ends, set horizontal speed to the normal max speed in the same direction.
- The next dash is allowed 200 ms after the previous one ends.
- Show readiness with a small meter or a color change.

Acceptance checks:
1. Mashing dash gives at most one dash per 150 + 200 ms.
2. A press during the cooldown does nothing, and the meter shows why.
3. Dash distance is the same at 30 and 144 fps.
