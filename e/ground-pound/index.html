Ground pound

Also called: butt stomp, hip drop, slam, dive bomb, stomp attack

A button in the air that stops the player dead, holds them for a moment, then drops them straight down fast to break or hit what's below.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · ↓ or S down

Try this: You start on a stack of crates. Jump, then press Down in the air. You stop, hang for a moment, then slam down and break the crate you land on. Turn it off and Down in the air does nothing, so the crates stay put.

What it does

Fast fall just speeds up a fall that's already happening. A ground pound is a move you commit to. Frame Advance's reading of the Super Mario 64 code says pressing Z in the air stops Mario dead, lifts him up to 110 units over 10 frames, holds him until his spin ends, then drops him starting at 50 units a frame. Banjo-Kazooie's Beak Buster hangs too, then pops up a little before it falls. The pause shows a big hit is coming, and the straight drop makes it easy to aim at a block or an enemy below.

Use it when

Platformers with breakable floors, switches to press or enemies to hit from above. It also gives players a quick way out of a jump they don't like.

Watch out

Once it starts the player can't steer, so a pound that misses should still feel fair. Make the hang easy to see and keep it short. Down in the air may already mean fast fall or drop through a platform, so pick one meaning.

Starting values

Super Mario 64 rises over 10 frames, then holds until the spin animation ends plus 4 frames. No checked source gives the spin's length. This demo hangs for 200 ms, then drops at 1100 px/s, about 1.5 times its normal 720 px/s max fall.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add a ground pound.
- Pressing [down / the pound button] in the air starts it. Stop all movement right away.
- Hold the player in place for 200 ms. Show it with a spin, a pose or a sound.
- Then drop straight down at [1.5] times the normal max fall speed. No steering during the drop.
- On landing, hit or break whatever is right below and play a landing effect like dust or a small shake.
- It can't start on the ground or during another pound.

Acceptance checks:
1. Starting a pound stops horizontal movement on the same frame.
2. The drop starts 200 ms after the press. Log it.
3. A pound that lands on a breakable block breaks it. A normal landing doesn't.
4. Pressing the button on the ground does nothing.
