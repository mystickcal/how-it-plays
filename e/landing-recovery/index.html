Landing recovery

Also called: landing lag, hard landing, heavy landing, landing stun

After a hard landing, the character can't act for a few frames, which gives falls weight.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump

Try this: Walk off the high ledge and hold right as you land. Then hop on the ground: small hops have no recovery.

What it does

A character who can run at full speed the instant they hit the ground after a long fall feels weightless. Landing recovery locks movement and jumping for a short time after any landing faster than a threshold. Small hops stay free, so normal play is not slowed down. A squash on the landing frame shows the player why they are stuck.

Use it when

Games that want weight: brawlers, fighting games, heavy characters.

Watch out

Precise platformers, where any lost control feels unfair. Keep it short and only on hard landings.

Starting values

SmashWiki's landing lag page lists light landings at 2 frames and heavy landings from 2 to 6 frames in later Smash games, about 33 to 100 ms at 60 fps.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add landing recovery after hard landings.
- On the frame the player lands, if their downward speed was at least 600 px/s, block movement and jumping for 100 ms (6 frames at 60 fps).
- Lighter landings get no recovery.
- Squash the sprite on the landing frame so the player sees why.

Acceptance checks:
1. Dropping from a high ledge stops the player for about 100 ms.
2. A normal hop on flat ground never triggers it.
3. The timing is the same at 30 and 144 fps.
