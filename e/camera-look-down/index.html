Camera look-down

Also called: look down, peek camera, crouch to look, manual camera offset

Holding down while standing still pans the camera down after a short delay, so the player can check a drop before taking it.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · ↓ or S down

Try this: Stand at the edge of the top ledge and hold down. Let go and it pans back.

What it does

Players hate jumping into a pit they can't see. Letting them hold down to peek uses an input they already have. A short delay keeps a quick tap of down from moving the camera. Itay Keren's camera talk points to Spelunky, where crouching or looking up shifts the camera, as a good use of controls already in the game.

Use it when

Levels with drops that go below the screen.

Watch out

Don't pan while the player is moving, or it fights the normal follow.

Starting values

No checked source gives numbers. 100 to 150 px of pan after holding for about 300 ms is a starting point.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add a look-down camera.
- While the player is on the ground, nearly still, and has held down for at least 300 ms, ease a camera offset to 120 px downward.
- Otherwise ease it back to 0.
- Ease with offset += (target - offset) * (1 - Math.exp(-6 * dt)).
- Clamp the camera to the level bounds.

Acceptance checks:
1. Holding down for 300 ms pans the view down by 120 px.
2. A quick tap of down doesn't move the camera.
3. Walking cancels the pan.
