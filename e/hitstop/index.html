Hitstop

Also called: hit pause, freeze frames, hit lag, impact freeze

Freeze the action for a few frames when a hit lands so the impact registers.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · X or J attack

Try this: Hit the dummy with X at 0 ms, then 80 ms, then 200 ms.

What it does

When a hit connects and everything keeps moving, the eye barely registers contact. A short freeze on the hit frame gives it weight. Too long and the game feels like it's stuttering.

Use it when

Melee hits, heavy impacts, parries, the last hit of a combo.

Watch out

Rapid-fire weapons that hit many times a second, where freezes would stack into lag.

Starting values

50 to 100 ms (3 to 6 frames at 60 fps) for normal hits, longer for heavy ones. Celeste's player code freezes the game for 0.05 seconds on some actions.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add hitstop when an attack connects.
- On hit, set hitstopRemaining = max(hitstopRemaining, 80 ms). Don't add freezes together.
- While hitstopRemaining > 0, skip gameplay updates (movement, physics, animation timers) but keep reading input and keep audio playing.
- Count down in real time.
- Presses made during the freeze still take effect after it.

Acceptance checks:
1. One hit freezes the action for about 80 ms.
2. Ten quick hits never produce one long freeze.
3. A jump pressed during the freeze happens right after it.
