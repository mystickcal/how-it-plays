Cancel windows

Also called: combo window, attack cancel, hit confirm cancel, chain window

After an attack connects, a short window lets the next attack start right away, skipping the rest of the recovery.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · X or J attack

Try this: Hit the dummy and press X again quickly. With cancels on, the hits chain into a combo. Swing at the air and the full recovery stays.

What it does

Long recovery makes a missed attack risky, which is good, but it also makes landed hits slow to follow up. A cancel window opens only when the attack hit something: a press inside it starts the next attack at once. Misses keep the full recovery, so landing hits is rewarded and swinging at nothing is not.

Use it when

Combo-based combat, fighting games, character action games.

Watch out

Too wide a window lets mashing do everything. Keep it to the moment right after a hit.

Starting values

Infil's fighting game glossary defines a cancel as removing an attack's recovery to go straight into another move, usually after it hits. This demo uses a 150 ms window inside 300 ms of recovery as a starting point.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add cancel windows to attacks.
- When an attack hits, open a cancel window for the first 150 ms of its recovery.
- Pressing attack inside the window starts the next attack immediately.
- If the attack missed, there is no window and the full recovery plays.
- Track a combo counter that resets after 600 ms without a hit.

Acceptance checks:
1. Hit, then press attack within 150 ms: the next attack starts on that frame.
2. A missed attack can't be cancelled.
3. A press outside the window does nothing until recovery ends.
