Knockback

Also called: hit push, pushback, launch, hit reaction

A hit pushes the target away from the attacker with a small upward pop, then friction slows it down.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · X or J attack

Try this: Hit the dummy with X. Change the force and friction and see how far it slides.

What it does

If a target doesn't react to being hit, it reads as if the attack missed. Knockback sets the target's velocity away from the attacker on the hit frame and lets friction bring it to a stop. It pairs well with hitstop: freeze first, then launch.

Use it when

Melee and projectile hits, explosions, and also the player getting hit.

Watch out

Puzzle games where position must stay exact, or near walls and pits unless you want that to be part of the game.

Starting values

Force 250 to 400 px/s with an upward pop around 40 to 50 percent of that, friction 1000 to 2000 px/s².

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add knockback when an attack hits an enemy.
- On hit: enemy.vx = attackerFacing * 340 px/s, enemy.vy = -340 * 0.45.
- While the enemy is on the ground, move vx toward 0 by 1400 px/s² * dt.
- The enemy can't act until it stops sliding (hitstun).
- If hitstop is in the game, apply knockback after the freeze ends.

Acceptance checks:
1. A hit always pushes the enemy away from the attacker, from both sides.
2. The slide distance is the same at 30 and 144 fps.
3. Knockback into a wall stops cleanly with no clipping into it.
