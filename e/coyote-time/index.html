Coyote time

Also called: jump grace time, ledge forgiveness, late jump window

For a short moment after the player runs off a ledge, jump still works as if they were on the ground.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · X or J attack

Try this: Run right and press jump just after you leave each edge. Turn it off and try the same thing.

What it does

Players press jump a few frames late all the time, mostly right at the edge of a platform. Without coyote time the game sees that press as "in the air" and ignores it, and the player falls while sure they pressed jump. With it, you track how long ago the player last stood on ground and allow a normal jump while that time is under the window.

Use it when

Any platformer with gaps or ledges.

Watch out

Never give it after a jump has already started, or it turns into a free double jump.

Starting values

Around 100 ms is a common starting point. Celeste's public player code uses a 0.1 second jump grace time. Try 60 to 150 ms.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add coyote time to the player's jump.
- Track the time since the player was last on the ground.
- Allow a normal ground jump while that time is under 100 ms, even if the player is no longer touching the ground.
- Only after walking off an edge. Once any jump starts, clear the timer so coyote time can't give a second jump.
- Put the window in a named constant (COYOTE_MS = 100) and use real time (delta time), not frame counts.

Acceptance checks:
1. Walking off an edge and pressing jump 50 ms later jumps.
2. Pressing jump 150 ms after leaving the edge does nothing.
3. Jumping, then pressing jump again in the air, never gives a second jump.
