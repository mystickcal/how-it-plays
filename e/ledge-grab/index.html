Ledge grab

Also called: ledge hang, ledge catch, mantle, ledge climb

When a jump ends with the player's head level with a ledge they are pushing into, they catch it and hang, then jump to climb up.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · ↓ or S down

Try this: Jump at the tall block on the right while holding right. It is just too high to land on. With ledge grab on you catch it; press jump to climb.

What it does

Players judge height badly, and missing a ledge by a few pixels is a common frustration. A ledge grab checks, while falling and holding toward a wall, whether the top corner of that wall is near the player's head. If it is, the player snaps to hang there. Jump climbs up, down lets go. A near miss becomes a success without making every jump higher.

Use it when

Adventure and action platformers, especially with realistic jump heights.

Watch out

Precision platformers where exact jump height is the challenge. Block a new grab for a moment after letting go, or the player grabs again at once.

Starting values

No checked source gives numbers. A grab zone from about 10 px above to 8 px below the top of the head, and 300 ms before another grab after dropping, are starting points to tune.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add a ledge grab.
- While in the air, falling, and holding toward a wall the player touches: if the top of that wall is between 10 px above and 8 px below the top of the player's head, snap the player to hang with their head just above the ledge. Set speed to 0 and stop gravity.
- While hanging: jump climbs up with a normal jump, down lets go.
- After letting go, don't allow another grab for 300 ms.

Acceptance checks:
1. A jump that ends just below a ledge catches it.
2. Pressing jump while hanging ends with the player standing on top.
3. Pressing down drops the player, who does not grab again right away.
