Moving platforms that carry the player

Also called: platform riding, carry momentum, lift boost, moving platform carry

A player standing on a moving platform moves with it, and a jump off it keeps the platform's speed.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump

Try this: Stand still on the green-topped platform. Turn carrying off and it slides away under you.

What it does

If a moving platform is only a solid box, it slides out from under the player, who has to walk to keep up. The fix: before moving characters, find who stands on each platform, move the platform, then move those riders by the same amount. Celeste also adds the platform's speed to the player's when they jump off (its code calls this lift boost), so a jump from a fast platform carries.

Use it when

Any moving solid the player can stand on: lifts, carts, conveyor parts.

Watch out

Cap the inherited speed so a fast platform can't fling the player across the level.

Starting values

Rodrigo Monteiro's platformer guide gives the order: find riders, step platforms, shift riders by the platform's movement, then step characters. Celeste's public player code caps the inherited speed at 250 sideways and 130 upward (at 320 by 180).

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Make moving platforms carry the player.
- Each frame, before moving the player, check whether they stand on a moving platform (bottom edge within 0.5 px of its top, overlapping horizontally).
- Move the platforms, then move each rider by the platform's change in position.
- When the player jumps off, add the platform's velocity to theirs, capped to a sensible max.
- In this demo the platform makes one round trip every 4 s.

Acceptance checks:
1. Standing still on a moving platform keeps the player on it.
2. Jumping off a moving platform carries its speed for a moment.
3. Walking on the platform works in both directions.
