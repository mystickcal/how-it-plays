Double jump

Also called: air jump, extra jump, multi-jump

Allow one extra jump in the air, refilled when the player lands.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump

Try this: Jump, then press jump again near the top. Try to reach the highest platform with it off.

What it does

A second jump lets players fix a jump that went wrong and reach higher places. Keep a counter of air jumps left, refill it on landing, and spend one when jump is pressed in the air. A coyote jump off a ledge should count as the ground jump, not use up the air jump.

Use it when

Games about exploration or recovery, where reaching higher spots is the reward.

Watch out

Every gap and ledge now has to account for the extra height, which makes levels harder to design. Precise platformers often leave it out.

Starting values

Rodrigo Monteiro's platformer guide describes the counter approach and notes that the second jump is sometimes shorter. 0.8 to 1.0 times the first jump's speed is a sensible starting range.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add a double jump.
- Keep airJumpsLeft. Set it to 1 whenever the player is on the ground.
- When jump is pressed in the air, a normal or coyote jump is not allowed, and airJumpsLeft > 0: set vertical speed to 0.85 times the normal jump speed (upward) and decrease airJumpsLeft.
- Each press can do only one jump.

Acceptance checks:
1. Jump, then jump again in the air: the second jump works.
2. A third press in the air does nothing.
3. Landing refills the extra jump.
