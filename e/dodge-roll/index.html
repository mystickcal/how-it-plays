Dodge roll

Also called: evade roll, roll i-frames, dodge invulnerability, evasive roll

A quick roll that can't be hurt for its length, so a well-timed roll passes straight through an attack.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · C K or Shift roll

Try this: Press C (or K, or Shift) to roll through the red ball as it comes at you. Turn invincibility off and the same roll gets hit.

What it does

A roll that only moves the player is just a slow dash. Making it immune to damage turns it into a defensive tool: time it right and the attack goes through you. The cost is commitment. The roll has a fixed length and a cooldown, and a badly timed roll leaves you open.

Use it when

Action games with attacks meant to be dodged.

Watch out

Immunity for the whole roll with no cooldown makes it a permanent shield. Add a cooldown or a vulnerable tail at the end.

Starting values

No checked source gives roll timings. 250 to 400 ms of roll with a cooldown of 200 to 400 ms are starting points. Infil's fighting game glossary describes invincible moves and notes that many games make backdashes briefly invincible.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add a dodge roll with invincibility.
- On the dodge button: roll in the held direction (or facing) at 380 px/s for 300 ms.
- During the roll the player can't take damage.
- Another roll is allowed 250 ms after the previous one ends.
- Show the invincible part, for example by brightening the sprite.

Acceptance checks:
1. Rolling through a projectile takes no damage.
2. Getting hit right after the roll ends does damage.
3. Mashing can't chain rolls faster than one per 300 + 250 ms.
