Variable jump height

Also called: jump cut, short hop, hold to jump higher

Tap jump for a short hop, hold it for a full jump. When the player lets go while still rising, cut the upward speed.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · X or J attack

Try this: Tap jump, then hold it. The dotted trail shows each arc.

What it does

With one fixed jump, small hops are impossible and the player spends a long time in the air for every tiny step, which reads as floaty. The common fix: when jump is released and the character is still moving up, multiply the vertical speed by a number under 1. Gravity then ends the jump sooner.

Use it when

Platformers where the player needs both small and big jumps.

Watch out

Games where every jump should be identical on purpose, like some puzzle or rhythm games.

Starting values

A cut of 0.4 to 0.5 is a common start. Celeste does it differently: it keeps the jump speed for up to 0.2 seconds while the button is held. Both work.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add variable jump height.
- Jump starts with an upward speed of 480 px/s.
- When the jump button is released while the player is still moving up, multiply vertical speed by 0.45.
- Apply the cut only once per jump and only while rising.

Acceptance checks:
1. A quick tap peaks clearly lower than a full hold. Log both peak heights.
2. Releasing the button while falling changes nothing.
3. A full hold reaches the same height as before this change.
