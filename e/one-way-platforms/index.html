One-way platforms with drop-through

Also called: jump-through platforms, pass-through platforms, semisolid platforms, drop through

Platforms you can jump up through from below and land on from above, with down plus jump to drop back through.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · ↓ or S down (with jump: drop through)

Try this: Jump up through the platforms from below. Stand on one and press down and jump together to drop.

What it does

Solid platforms stop the player's head on the way up, so a stack of them becomes a maze. A one-way platform only blocks a body that was fully above its top edge before this frame's move and is moving down. Checking the previous position matters: a player who jumps halfway into the platform and falls back should drop through, not snap onto it. Dropping through ignores one-way platforms for a moment.

Use it when

Stacked platforms, multi-level arenas, anything the player should reach from below.

Watch out

Keep the drop input distinct so it never fires by accident, for example down plus jump rather than down alone.

Starting values

Rodrigo Monteiro's platformer guide gives the rule: the platform counts only if the player's bottom was above its top before the move. Ignoring one-way platforms for about 200 ms on a drop is a starting point to tune. Celeste's code has its own version, called JumpThru.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add one-way platforms.
- They never block horizontal movement.
- They block vertical movement only when the player's bottom edge was at or above the platform's top before this frame's move, and the player is moving down.
- Drop-through: when the player stands on a one-way platform and presses down and jump together, ignore one-way platforms for 200 ms and do not jump.

Acceptance checks:
1. Jumping from below passes through and lands on top.
2. A jump that only reaches halfway through falls back down through it.
3. Down plus jump on a one-way platform drops through. On solid ground it does a normal jump.
