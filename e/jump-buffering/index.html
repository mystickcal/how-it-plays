Jump buffering

Also called: jump buffer, early jump press, buffered jump input

If the player presses jump just before landing, remember the press for a short time and jump the moment they touch the ground.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump · X or J attack

Try this: Jump, then press jump again a moment before you land. With the buffer off, early presses do nothing.

What it does

When players chain jumps they often press a little early, while still falling. Without a buffer that press is thrown away because they weren't on the ground yet, and the game feels like it ignored them. With a buffer you store when jump was pressed, and on each frame you jump if the player is grounded and the press is recent enough.

Use it when

Any game with repeated jumps, and the same idea works for dashes and other moves.

Watch out

Keep the window short. A long buffer makes the character jump when the player has already changed their mind.

Starting values

100 to 150 ms is a common start. Celeste's player code reads jump from a buffered input and consumes the buffer when a jump starts.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Add jump buffering.
- When jump is pressed, store the time of the press.
- Every frame, if the player is grounded and a jump press happened in the last 120 ms, jump and clear the stored press.
- Only new presses count. Holding the button must not auto-repeat jumps.
- Use real time (delta time) for the window. JUMP_BUFFER_MS = 120.

Acceptance checks:
1. Pressing jump 60 ms before landing jumps on the frame the player lands.
2. Pressing jump 170 ms before landing does nothing.
3. Holding jump through a landing does not trigger a second jump.
