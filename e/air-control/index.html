Air control

Also called: aerial acceleration, air steering, air strafing, mid-air control

Let the player steer left and right during a jump, usually with weaker acceleration than on the ground.

←→ or AD move · Space W ↑ or Z jump

Try this: Run right, jump, then press left in mid-air. Turn air control off and try to land on the small platforms.

What it does

With no air control, the direction at takeoff decides the whole jump, which feels stiff and rules out small corrections. With air control, horizontal input keeps working in the air, using the ground acceleration times a multiplier. That multiplier sets how much the player can correct a jump once it has started.

Use it when

Nearly every platformer.

Watch out

Games where committing to a jump is the point, like some cinematic platformers.

Starting values

Celeste's public player code multiplies run acceleration by 0.65 in the air. Game Maker's Toolkit points out that Madeline has so much air friction that she falls almost straight down when you let go of the stick.

Prompt for your coding agent

The numbers follow the sliders above. Works with plain canvas, Phaser, Three.js or Godot.

Give the player air control.
- In the air, use the ground horizontal acceleration and deceleration multiplied by 0.65.
- Keep the same max speed in the air.
- Use delta time.

Acceptance checks:
1. Reversing direction in mid-air visibly bends the jump.
2. Letting go in the air slows horizontal speed at 0.65 times the ground rate.
3. Ground movement is unchanged.
